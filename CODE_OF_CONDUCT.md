# Code of Conduct

Wardscript follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticize ideas and not people, and
don't harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, @ahmedelraei, privately. Reports
are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or
block people from the project, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
